The city transit museum catalog lists a 1924 brass subway token with an oak-leaf mint mark on the reverse, accession number T-1924-B. The obverse shows a simplified car silhouette and the legend CITY LINES. Counterfeit zinc pieces from the 1970s lack the oak-leaf and weigh 1.1 grams less than the brass original. Tokens are stored in acid-free envelopes, one piece per envelope, inside a dry cabinet at 40 percent relative humidity.
