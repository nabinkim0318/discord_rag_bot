The bakery maintains a rye levain at a 1:2:2 feeding ratio of starter, water, and dark rye flour. Peak activity arrives about eight hours after feeding when the culture is held at 26 degrees Celsius. Bakers look for a domed surface and a clean, yogurt-like aroma; a sharp acetone smell means the levain is hungry and should be fed again before mixing dough. Discard is baked into crackers rather than poured down the drain.
