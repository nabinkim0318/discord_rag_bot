The Northhaven harbor lantern sits on the granite mole and is lit every evening at civil dusk. Its reservoir is filled with rendered whale oil, never kerosene, because the original 1887 wick assembly scorches on petroleum distillates. Keepers trim the wick to four millimeters and record the lighting time in the harbor log. In fog the lantern stays burning until nautical dawn even if the automatic sun-switch would otherwise extinguish it.
