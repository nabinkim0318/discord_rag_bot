Studio kiln firing for mid-fire stoneware uses cone 6 oxidation. The controller holds the chamber at 1220 degrees Celsius for a fifteen-minute soak so feldspar in the clay body can melt without bloating. Do not open the spy hole during the soak; a cold draft will craze the glaze. After the soak, cool at 150 degrees Celsius per hour until 700, then allow natural cooling to room temperature before unloading shelves.
