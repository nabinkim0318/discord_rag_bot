Field notes on Gentiana acaulis record it on limestone scree above 1800 meters, never on granite soils. Conservation staff collect ripe capsules in late August and deposit seed in the alpine seed bank at minus 18 degrees Celsius. Transplanting flowering rosettes from the scree fails; nursery plants must be raised from banked seed in a gritty calcareous mix. Grazing sheep are fenced out of the marked plots from June through September.
