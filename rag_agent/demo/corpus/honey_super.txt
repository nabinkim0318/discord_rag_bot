During the late-June nectar flow, beekeepers add a Langstroth honey super above a queen excluder so the queen cannot lay in the harvest boxes. Each super holds ten frames of drawn comb. Harvest begins when at least seven frames are capped. Smoke is used sparingly at the hive entrance; heavy smoke taints the clover honey. Wet supers are returned to the colony the same evening so the bees can clean residual nectar.
